# Compact output template

## Contribution as understood

One sentence. If the first-read surfaces do not support a stable restatement, say so.

## Overall assessment

One short paragraph balancing the central strength and the concern that most controls the judgment.

## Concrete strengths

Zero to three numbered items. Each names a specific method, result, comparison, or presentation success.

## Decision-changing concerns

Zero to three numbered items. For each include location, observed fact, reviewer inference, decision impact, and proportionate resolution in compact prose.

## Clarification questions

Zero to three genuine questions. Omit this section when none are material.

## Provisional ICRA recommendation, if requested

- Band: current official ICRA band.
- Confidence: low, medium, or high, with one reason.
- Controlling issue: the single concern most responsible for the band, or “none identified.”
- What would move the band: one evidence change or clarification, not a long fix list.

Omit this section when no score or recommendation was requested. For a revision comparison, identify the changed evidence or resolved misunderstanding instead of presenting AI-score movement as validation. Keep presentation findings distinct from scientific defects without adding a separate compulsory checklist.

## Review limits

State missing artifacts, domain limits, lack of fresh context, or unverified literature that materially constrain the assessment.
