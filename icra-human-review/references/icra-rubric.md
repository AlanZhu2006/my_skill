# ICRA rubric and policy

Always verify the target year's live official page before using these notes.

Official source checked 2026-09-06: [IEEE RAS Information for ICRA Reviewers](https://www.ieee-ras.org/conferences-workshops/fully-sponsored/icra/information-for-icra-reviewers/).

## Current recommendation bands

- A / 5.0: definitely accept; excellent paper, advocate for acceptance.
- B+ / 4.5: accept; strong advocacy.
- B / 4.0: high borderline, leaning accept.
- B- / 3.5: borderline.
- C / 3.0: low borderline, leaning reject.
- C- / 2.5: reject; substantial improvement required.
- D / 2.0: definitely reject; trivial, wrong, known, or clearly below conference quality.
- U / 1.0: inappropriate; not a fifth technical grade.

The official page requests a detailed review that describes the paper's contribution and justifies the overall assessment. It also explicitly rejects meaningless or repeated text used merely to meet the character threshold.

## AI boundary

The current official page states that information or content in or about a manuscript under review shall not be processed through an AI system. This skill is therefore for author-side pre-submission review of authorized material. Do not use it for a formally assigned confidential review.

## Scoring discipline

- Write the scientific judgment before choosing a band.
- Give one or two reasons that control the band.
- Report confidence separately.
- Do not invent acceptance probabilities or unofficial weighted score formulas.
- Do not import another study's reject-first prior, rating caps, or ICLR-style score threshold into ICRA. A stricter prompt is a changed evaluation condition, not evidence that its rating is more accurate.
- Provide a numerical rating or recommendation band only when requested; verify the target rubric at that time.
