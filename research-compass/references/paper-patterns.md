# Casebook: infer research moves, not modules

Reviewed 2026-09-27. These examples are a **dated reading aid**, not a current literature review, priority ranking, or account of how the authors privately found their ideas. “Research move” is our inference from each primary paper's problem formulation, design, and evidence. Verify current papers, implementations, and newer equivalents before making a transfer or novelty claim. This file is optional and must never set the target of an unrelated project.

| Move | Illustrative primary work | Transferable question and important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove an unnecessary prediction burden | [ABot-Recon](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27529) predicts current-frame pointmaps and adjacent pose from a short local context, then composes global estimates. | Is a growing reference frame or target range making the learned problem harder than necessary? Fixed local prediction does not by itself solve global revisits or remove composition drift; the paper also evaluates an optional loop-closure backend. |
| Seek sufficient variables rather than many task heads | [Depth Anything 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.10647) builds around depth and ray maps as geometric targets, while retaining selected cross-view attention and substantial data work. | Can the required outputs be derived from a smaller representation? Do not attribute all reported gains to target choice, or equate a plain backbone with no specialized interaction. |
| Ask whether adaptation has fewer degrees of freedom than the model | [LoRA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09685) freezes pretrained weights and trains low-rank updates, with empirical analysis of rank deficiency. | Is the task-specific change effectively low-dimensional, and what rank is sufficient under the desired conditions? Do not assume every task or layer shares that property. |
| Give different information different roles and lifetimes | [LingBot-Map](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14141) distinguishes anchor, local pose-reference window, and compact trajectory context. | What must persist, what must stay dense, and what can be summarized? Its trajectory tokens accumulate over time; compact is not the same as constant-size. |
| Compress only interactions shown to be redundant | [VGGT-Ω](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.15195) partially replaces global attention with register attention; replacing all incurs a larger accuracy cost. | Which interactions need fine-grained exchange? Is the measured effect from selective routing rather than changed data, backbone, or heads? Use the targeted ablation for the narrow claim. |
| Reidentify the true resource bottleneck | [FlashAttention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.14135) treats memory traffic, not only arithmetic complexity, as central and uses tiling for exact attention. | Is the real bottleneck data movement, synchronization, or another cost the usual complexity metric hides? Hardware assumptions and wall-clock measurement matter; an asymptotic argument alone is insufficient. |
| Align the learning signal with what downstream use must preserve | [LingBot-Vision](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05247) makes boundary structure a pretraining signal for dense spatial perception. | Does a common training invariant erase detail required by the actual task? A boundary loss is not automatically valuable where boundary detail is irrelevant. |
| Derive outputs from the downstream contract | [Instant NuRec](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.14203) produces layered static/dynamic Gaussian scenes, sky, and camera corrections for simulation from calibrated multi-camera logs. | What must the consumer render, edit, or control? Its calibrated camera rig and poses are substantive privileges, not a fair direct comparison with pure-RGB inference. |
| Transfer a structural principle, not its surface form | [ResNet](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385) reformulates deep layers as residual functions around identity paths to ease optimization. | Does the recipient have an aligned quantity that can bypass the same bottleneck? A temporal memory problem may first require retrieval and coordinate alignment; “add skip connections” is not yet a transfer. |
| Let a theory generate a method, then challenge its attribution | [ChordEdit](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.19083) uses a low-energy transport view to derive a smoothed one-step editing field. A [reproduction](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.14042) finds a matched effective-timestep simplification gives similar results in its tested setting. | Does the borrowed principle add value beyond timing, smoothing, or a retuned baseline? The reproduction narrows that mechanism claim; it does not prove universal equivalence or that the algorithm is useless. |
| Check the nearest integrated solution before combining known parts | [Adaptive World Memory 3D Foundation Model](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.21502) combines memory, submaps, loop closure, and Gaussian output with reported ablations. | Which *specific* coupling or tradeoff remains unsolved under comparable inputs? This recent preprint is not proof that every alternative is redundant; verify code availability before reproduction claims. |

The portable method is to ask which hidden burden was changed: **target, degrees of freedom, information lifetime, interaction granularity, true resource cost, learning signal, downstream contract, or explanatory theory**. These are search lenses, not a menu of modules. A candidate earns research value only when the recipient has a consequential matching tension, the source's assumptions map, and a comparison can separate the imported mechanism from an ordinary fix.
