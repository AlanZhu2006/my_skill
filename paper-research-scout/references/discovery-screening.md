# Open discovery: paper merit before project fit

## Default interests for this personal skill collection

Unless the user supplies a different scope, prioritize navigation, computer
vision, perception, Gaussian representations, 3D reconstruction and
SLAM/localization/mapping as one core band. Examples include visual/VLN/goal
navigation, active perception, geometry and semantics, correspondence,
depth/pose/tracking, visual representations, dynamic scenes and neural rendering.
These examples guide coverage, not a closed keyword filter.

Give manipulation (including mobile/dexterous/bimanual manipulation), humanoid
control, VLA and robot world models lower default attention. A particularly
original, consequential result or structurally useful outside idea can earn
inclusion. Generic perception/navigation vocabulary in a manipulation paper
does not itself establish a core contribution. Do not manufacture topic quotas.

Do not default to CEC or any other existing personal architecture. Explicit
requests to improve a named project are valid and change the scope; merely
discovering its notes, hardware or pending experiments does not. Research
Compass supplies a way of reasoning, not a requirement to inherit an old
project's research question.

## Coarse screening seeks reasons to read, not certified breakthroughs

Screen the collection before settling on directions. Use title/abstract evidence
to identify a potentially meaningful change in problem formulation, mechanism,
representation, assumption, capability or explanation. Record a read/watch/pass
decision and a concise reason. A keyword count, fresh date, famous author or
headline gain is not that reason. Keep abstract claims explicitly provisional.

Look for the strongest credible consequence as a separate axis: improved
accuracy/geometry/robustness/generalization, a cost-quality tradeoff, a new
capability, an informative negative finding, or a measurement that changes a
field's understanding. Do not compare percentages across incompatible tasks or
discard a conceptual advance simply because it lacks SOTA numbers.

A small provisional shortlist should expose the uncertainty worth resolving
by deep reading. Record it before project fit or idea construction; revise it
when new paper evidence warrants, with the reason. A bold but weakly supported
claim can remain a watch item; a practical but routine open method may be a
useful test vehicle without being a leading scientific contribution.

## Deep reading checks whether the promise survives

Trace the result to its metric, split, comparator and experimental setup. Check
data/supervision/compute differences, ablations and uncertainty where available.
Distinguish a matched comparison from a gain bundled with extra resources or
changed evaluation. Identify the closest primary prior work and what actually
differs; report unresolved novelty instead of declaring a first-of-its-kind.

Preserve three judgments: conceptual contribution, evidence/consequence, and
readiness for a particular intervention. Artifact auditing belongs to the
third. Code absence need not eliminate a conceptual lead; a README or download
link does not establish that a baseline runs or supports training.

Only then derive zero to a few opportunities. One source can suffice. A
cross-paper connection must earn its place through a shared structure or
assumption, not a requirement to combine modules. The best next action may be
a literature check or derivation rather than an experiment.
